Parent corner

Visual learning: diagrams and interactive apps can make complex math concepts much easier to grasp.

Hands-on science: simple science kits can turn nervousness about a subject into curiosity.

Balance: time for play and rest helps students stay focused during study hours.

Educators' insights

Role-play: playing characters helps students get past a fear of speaking in class.

Debate: structured debates build critical thinking and confidence.

Flexible classrooms: adapting to different learning styles keeps more students engaged.

Rethinking homework

Practical, creative activities at home, done with family, can teach as much as worksheets, and keep learning fun.
